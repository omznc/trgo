# trgo browser extension: privacy policy

The trgo extension collects no data.

- It has no analytics and no tracking.
- It loads no remote code.
- It sends nothing to the trgo developer.
- It stores nothing about you.

## What the extension does

The extension fetches marketplace pages for the trgo page. It does this
only when the trgo page asks. The request goes directly from your
browser to the marketplace. It does not go through a trgo server.

The extension reads only these sites:

- api.olx.ba
- svex.ba, listing.api.svex.ba and search.api.svex.ba
- www.kupujemprodajem.com
- www.njuskalo.hr

The extension answers only the trgo pages (trgo.omarzunic.com and
trgo.omznc.workers.dev). No other page can use it.

## What the trgo page does with the pages

The extension gives each page to the trgo page in the same browser tab.
The trgo page then shows the listings to you. It also sends the text of
the listings to the AI model that answers you, through OpenRouter:

- With your own OpenRouter key, the text goes from your browser to
  OpenRouter.
- In the free mode, the text goes through the trgo proxy to OpenRouter.
  The proxy keeps no logs and stores no text.

The extension itself does not do this. It only fetches the pages. The
trgo page has its own privacy notes under "What do we store?" in its
settings.

## Cookies

Only requests to www.njuskalo.hr carry your own cookies, and only for
search pages. The site needs them to accept the request. The extension
does not read, copy or store the cookies.

Requests to all other sites carry no cookies.
